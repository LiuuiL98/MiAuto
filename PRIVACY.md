# MiAuto privacy

Updated: 13 October 2026.

MiAuto runs on the car's head unit and receives a screen projection from your phone over
ICCOA CarLink (Xiaomi CarWith). Maps, music and calls are supplied by the phone's own
applications and are subject to those applications' policies. Touch and supported vehicle
inputs are sent back to the phone.

Settings and pairing information are stored locally on the car. Diagnostic logs and exported
settings can contain device identifiers, network details or other contextual data; review
them before sharing them. MiAuto includes no advertising and no analytics service, and it
does not talk to any server operated by this project.

Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, nearby-devices and USB permissions support the connection. Location
permissions may be required for wireless discovery. Microphone access supports voice input.
Notification and background permissions support the projection UI and the connection service.

The download site is static and uses no analytics scripts. GitHub handles visits and
downloads according to its own policies.
